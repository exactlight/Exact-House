Facing Foreclosure? You Have Options

A foreclosure notice feels like the end, but it's actually a critical window. Wisconsin law gives you time to act. We specialize in buying homes from people in pre-foreclosure situations, allowing them to close before the foreclosure process completes.

Why it matters: A foreclosure on your credit report damages your ability to borrow for 7+ years. We help you avoid this by closing quickly, before the legal process finalizes. You stop the foreclosure, walk away with cash, and protect your credit.
