Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really buy my home with a tax lien on it? +
Yes, absolutely. We specialize in tax lien situations. We have experience working with tax authorities and understand the process. Your home's equity still has value—we'll make a fair offer.
How much will I owe after the sale? +
The tax lien is satisfied from the sale proceeds. Our offer reflects this reality. You'll know exactly what you're getting before we close—no surprises.
How fast can you close? +
We typically close in 3 days, though tax lien situations may require slightly longer for proper paperwork. Contact us for your specific timeline.
Will I still owe taxes after selling? +
The tax lien on the property is resolved through the sale. For tax debt beyond the lien, you should consult with a tax professional. We focus on buying your home and managing the lien resolution.
