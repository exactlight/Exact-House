The True Cost of Holding a Vacant Property

Many property owners inherit vacant houses, own second properties they can't rent, or hold properties waiting for "the right time" to sell. But every month, the costs keep mounting: property taxes, insurance, utilities even though no one lives there, landscaping to maintain appearance, potential theft or vandalism, and deterioration from weather and abandonment.

The math is simple: A $100,000 property might cost $1,000-1,500 per month in carrying costs. After 12 months, you've paid $12,000-18,000 to own it while waiting. We buy it immediately at a fair price, and the bleeding stops.
